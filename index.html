ABOUT TANISH

A gemstone & jewellery house rooted in Jaipur

Tanish Gemstones & Jewellery brings together a carefully chosen range of labgrown gemstones and handcrafted jewellery, presented with the same attention to detail that Jaipur's gem trade has been known for.

Every piece in our collection is selected with an eye for colour, clarity and character. We work closely with each customer to understand what they are looking for, offering guidance rather than a hurried sale.
